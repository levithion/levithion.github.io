Lumina: Local-First Multimodal Memory Search

SmolVLM, CLIP, MiniLM, Qdrant, FastAPI, pywebview · 2026

Private screenshot search by meaning, on-screen text, and time, entirely on-device. A single process captions each capture, stores dual 512D and 384D vectors in embedded Qdrant, and fuses three signals for retrieval, taking roughly ten seconds from screenshot to searchable.

Turns a PyTorch NCF model trained on 100K ratings, 9K movies, and 600 users into a personalized discovery product. A hybrid ranker blends NCF scores with TF-IDF title and genre similarity and popularity, behind a FastAPI backend with accounts, watchlists, and cold-start handling.
