02Multimodal retrieval2026

Lumina

Private memory search that makes thousands of screenshots findable by meaning, on-screen text, and time — entirely on-device.

A single local process captions each capture with SmolVLM, embeds dual CLIP and MiniLM vectors into an embedded Qdrant store, and serves hybrid three-signal fusion with live watchdog indexing and date-aware filtering through a pywebview window.
